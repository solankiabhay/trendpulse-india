About TrendPulse India

Built by a Developer. Driven by Curiosity. Made for Curious India.

Hello! I'm the independent developer behind TrendPulse India — a technology-driven project built with a simple idea: make it easier for people to discover what's happening across India and around the world.

I'm a software developer passionate about building useful digital products, exploring new technologies, and turning ideas into practical solutions. I created TrendPulse India to bring publicly available headlines and trending topics together in one place, with short, source-linked digests in both English and Hindi.

Why I Built TrendPulse

In today's fast-moving digital world, information comes from everywhere. I wanted to build a simple platform where readers could quickly explore trending stories, discover different perspectives, and visit the original publishers for complete coverage.

TrendPulse India is an independent, automated project. It does not conduct original reporting or replace news publishers. It helps readers discover stories and directs them to their original sources.
