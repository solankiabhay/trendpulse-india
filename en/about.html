About TrendPulse India

TrendPulse India is an automated website that gathers publicly available headlines and trend signals about India and presents them as short, source-linked digests in English and Hindi.

It does not produce original reporting. Every digest links to the publishers whose headlines it summarises, so you can read the full story at the source.

The site is run as a small project by its owner. See the Contact page to reach us.
