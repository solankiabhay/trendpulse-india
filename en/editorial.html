Editorial Policy

Articles here are not original research. They are digests compiled automatically from headlines and summaries in public RSS feeds.

  • Every digest links to the original reports.
  • A topic is published only if at least two different publishers cover it, or its feed summary adds enough distinct detail.
  • Misleading or sensational titles, rumours and unconfirmed claims, old or undated items, and near-duplicates of published topics are rejected.
  • We do not independently verify the sources. Read the original reports for full details.
